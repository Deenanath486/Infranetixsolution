Dedicated Internet

What is a Dedicated Leased Line?

A Dedicated Leased Line (DLL) is a private, point-to-point internet connection that is exclusively allocated to your business. Unlike shared broadband, the bandwidth is never shared with other users — you get the full speed 24x7.

It provides symmetrical speeds — meaning your upload speed equals your download speed. This is critical for businesses that rely on cloud services, VoIP, video conferencing, and real-time data transfers.

100% Dedicated Bandwidth

Your speed is never shared. Full bandwidth available at all times.

Symmetrical Speeds

Equal upload and download — critical for cloud, VoIP, and data backups.

99.9% Uptime SLA

Written SLA with uptime guarantee and penalty clauses for downtime.
