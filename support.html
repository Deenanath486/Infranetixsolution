Quick Troubleshooting

Try these steps before raising a ticket.

1
Restart Your Router

Switch off the router, wait 30 seconds, then power it back on. Wait 2 minutes for it to reconnect.

2
Check All Cables

Ensure all fiber/ethernet cables are firmly plugged in. Check for any visible damage.

3
Check Router LED Status

Green = Normal. Red/Blinking = Issue. No light = Power problem.

4
Test on Another Device

If only one device has issues, it may be a device-specific problem, not your internet connection.

5
Check Our Network Status

Call us at 7992297991 — we'll check if there's any network maintenance in your area.
